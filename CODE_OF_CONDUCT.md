# Code of Conduct

LifePete follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the issues, Discussions and pull requests of this repository, and anywhere
you represent the project.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and criticise ideas rather than people.
Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at ahmed.elghareeb@proton.me. Reports
are read in confidence, and the maintainer decides on the response, from a warning to
a permanent ban, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
