From AHM-74's decisions:

> Pricing. The seat price, the free tier for small teams, and whether
> contractors count as seats.

Commercial rather than technical, and it gates phase 4's per-seat billing
entirely — there is nothing to build until the model is chosen.

Two constraints the epic already fixes, worth not relitigating: a person's own
plan is separate and is never changed by joining or leaving an organisation,
and personal use stays free at the entry tier.

The contractor question is the one with a product consequence rather than just
a revenue one. A freelancer joining a client's organisation does so to let the
client see hours on the client's own projects; charging the client a full seat
for that, or charging the freelancer, both change who is willing to do it. The
epic's own research note — Vercel bills a seat only on approval — is the
closest precedent collected so far.

Also still unchecked from the epic's research: competitor prices so far come
from aggregators rather than the vendors' own pages. Worth verifying before
anchoring on them.
