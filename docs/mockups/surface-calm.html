Surface calm — the two whites, and the box-per-thing page

Two findings, both measured from the shipped tokens. One: the afternoon cloud is brighter than the paper and sits at 1.01–1.04:1 against it — no edge at all — while drifting on a 140–200 s loop, so a panel's boundary dissolves and moves. Two: a page is eight to ten separate white rectangles, so the eye crosses that unstable edge ten times down one screen. Decided: the sky-tinted cloud shipped as the one afternoon cloud, and the layout shipped as a preference — Settings → Appearance → Page layout, boxes or one sheet. The switches remain so the before can still be seen.
